# FLARE — 4-Minute Demo Script

> Total: 4 minutes. Rehearse 5×. Backup video recorded separately. Offline demo mode ready if venue Wi-Fi dies.

1. **Apollo 1 hook (30s).** Select the 🕯️ Apollo 1 preset — 16.7 psia, 100% O₂. Point at the map: *the entire trade space sits deep inside the fire zone.* "On January 27, 1967, a flash fire broke out in a cabin at 16.7 psia of pure oxygen — and spread within seconds. That atmosphere had an oxygen partial pressure 541% of sea-level normoxia. FLARE computes this — it doesn't guess it."

2. **The real trade (45s).** Switch to 🔴 Mars transit 8.2 psia / 34% O₂ — NASA's actual Exploration Atmosphere. Show the hazard vector drop and the crew-safe region. Then hover 7.5/40: *same crew pO₂ as 10/30 — but a higher fire vector.* "Two crew-equivalent atmospheres are not fire-equivalent. That's the compromise every mission designer faces."

3. **Where the data ends (45s).** Drag O₂ to 38%. Evidence labels flip to **Interpolated**; coverage drops; the boundary fades into the hatched extrapolation zone. "We show you where NASA's data ends. We don't color pixels where NASA has no measurements."

4. **WHY? (60s).** Click **WHY?** The AI Scientist streams an answer with citation chips — click one → a real Saffire/BASS-II experiment card with a **live NTRS link**. "Every AI statement is checked against our database in code — fabricated citations get caught and flagged, not shown."

5. **Graded, not claimed (45s).** Open the Validation panel. "Our engine is graded against NASA's own held-out experiments — here are the predictions and the misses, with explanations. We publish our accuracy; we don't just claim it."

6. **The Pareto close (15s).** Move the fire-safety vs EVA weight slider — the Pareto frontier highlights a different optimum. "We never say which atmosphere is best. We show the trade. Every number is labeled Observed, Interpolated, or Extrapolated — and the boundary bends where the physics actually bends: microgravity is not automatically safer."
